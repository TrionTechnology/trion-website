Questions people ask

What is Titan Legends?

Titan Legends is a 3D action game for iPhone, iPad and Android. You play a cute-but-mighty Titan — Rex, Kongar, Shimo and more — stomping through tiny towns and toy armies, picking skill cards to evolve your powers, turning GIANT and toppling huge bosses across 12 lands.

How much does Titan Legends cost?

Titan Legends is a one-time purchase of USD 9.99 (local prices vary by store region). You own the full game: there are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

Is Titan Legends suitable for kids?

Yes. It is cartoon action with no blood or gore, designed for players from about 6 years old up to adults. A Kid Mode for players under 13 adds a parent check before external links and social features, uses preset names only, and offers a break reminder. Settings include Assist mode, Reduce flashes and Reduce motion.

When will Titan Legends be released?

Titan Legends is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Store links will appear on this page on launch day.

Does Titan Legends need an internet connection?

The main campaign is single-player. Social features such as guilds and raid bosses use an online connection.

How is my data handled, and can I delete my account?

Titan Legends has no ads and no tracking or advertising SDKs, and we never sell your data. Sign-in with Google is optional and guests can play without it. Our server keeps only what the online features need (player ID, save, leaderboard, friends and guild data), and you can delete it any time in the game (Settings → Delete account) or on our account deletion page. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who makes Titan Legends?

Titan Legends is designed, built and published by Trion Creation Sdn Bhd in Malaysia.
