Privacy Policy

Titan Legends, by Trion Creation Sdn Bhd ("we", "us"), Malaysia.
Contact: freddy.chia@trioncreation.com
Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

Titan Legends is a paid game: you buy it once from the App Store or Google Play. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no advertising or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your data for advertising. To run cloud saves and the online features (leaderboard, friends, guilds, bosses and the arena), our server keeps a small game account for you. You can play as a guest without signing in, and you can delete your account and its data at any time in the game (Settings → Delete account) or on our account deletion page.

What we keep and why

Your game account (created automatically, also for guests):

  • a random player ID and a random install ID that the game creates (not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID);
  • your platform (iOS or Android), the display name you choose (in Kid Mode, a preset name such as "Brave Comet 27"), your friend code, and when the account was created and last used.

Sign-in, if you choose it. Sign-in with Google (and, later, with Apple on iOS) is optional. When you sign in, we store only the account identifier that Google or Apple gives the game for your account, so you can get your progress back on another device. We do not receive or store your name, email address, photo or contacts from Google or Apple.

Your save and progress: your game save (stages, Titans, items, settings and the birth year entered on the age screen, which decides the play mode), its version number, your deepest stage and hero level.

Online features:

  • your leaderboard position;
  • a snapshot of your Titan (its build and power) so friends and other players can see it and borrow it as a helper;
  • friends, friend requests and players you block;
  • guild membership, role and contributions;
  • World Boss, guild raid and arena results (damage, attempts, rating, wins and losses).

Reports: if you report another player, we store who reported whom, when, and the reason you wrote, so we can moderate.

Security: sign-in tokens, and server logs with your IP address and the request made, used to keep the service secure, limit abuse and fix problems.

Emails: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, no advertising IDs and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs from third parties.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No payment details: Apple or Google handle the purchase of the game. There are no in-app purchases.
  • No chat. Players see each other's display names, friend codes, Titans, guild names and rankings, nothing more.

Unity engine diagnostics. The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's own analytics, ads, purchasing, cloud diagnostics and performance reporting services are switched off. The engine's built-in hardware statistics and engine diagnostics are currently on, so the Unity engine may send Unity Technologies basic technical information about your device and how the engine runs (for example device model, operating system, graphics hardware and engine errors). Unity uses it to improve its engine; we do not use it to identify or track you. See Unity's policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Who can see your data

  • Other players can see your display name, friend code, Titan snapshot, leaderboard rank, guild membership and boss or arena rankings.
  • Our hosting provider, Alibaba Cloud, stores the data for us on servers in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. If you play from another country, your data is transferred to and processed in Malaysia.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in and the game purchase under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

We keep your game account and the data above while the account exists. When you delete your account, the player account, save, sign-in links, leaderboard entry, Titan snapshot, friends, blocks, guild membership, boss and arena records and reports are deleted from our database straight away. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting. Support emails are deleted within 12 months after the conversation ends, or sooner if you ask.

Deleting your account

  • In the game: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your online account and its data immediately, and the next launch starts as a new guest.
  • On the web: follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email freddy.chia@trioncreation.com.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your online account, so please use one of the options above.

Children

Titan Legends is made for players from 6 years old. The first time it matters, the game shows a neutral age screen that asks for a birth year, with nothing pre-selected. Players under 13 get Kid Mode:

  • a grown-up check (parental gate) before social features and before any link that leaves the game;
  • preset names only, so no real names appear on leaderboards or in guilds;
  • notifications off by default and an optional break reminder.

Children can play as a guest without signing in. We do not ask children for a name, email address, phone number or photo, and we keep only what is needed to run the game: the random IDs, the save (including the birth year from the age screen) and the preset name.

Parents: you can ask us to review or delete your child's data at any time by emailing freddy.chia@trioncreation.com (please include the friend code shown in the game), or delete it yourself in the game with Settings → Delete account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, the EU and UK GDPR and the California CCPA give you the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data and to object to its use. Email us to make a request; we will answer as the law requires. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens expire and are revoked when you delete your account.

Changes

If we change what data the game handles, we will update this policy and the store privacy labels before that version is released, and change the date above.
