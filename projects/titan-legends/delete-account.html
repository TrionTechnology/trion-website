Delete your Titan Legends account

This page explains how to delete your Titan Legends account and its data. Titan Legends is published by Trion Creation Sdn Bhd.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Titan Legends.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your online account and its data are deleted immediately. The game signs you out, and the next launch starts as a new guest.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the game)

  1. Email freddy.chia@trioncreation.com with the subject "Delete my Titan Legends account".
  2. Include your friend code (Friends screen, "YOUR FRIEND CODE") or your display name, the platform (iPhone or Android), and whether you signed in with Google or Apple.
  3. We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • your player account, install ID, display name and friend code;
  • the Google or Apple sign-in link;
  • your game save and progress;
  • your leaderboard entry and Titan snapshot;
  • friends, friend requests and blocks;
  • guild membership and contributions, World Boss, raid and arena records;
  • reports you made and reports made about you.

What is kept

  • Nothing in the game database. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Server logs (IP address and request details) are kept only for a short time for security, then removed.
  • If you email us, the conversation is deleted within 12 months after it ends.
  • Your purchase of the game is recorded by Apple or Google, not by us, so you can still re-download the game.

To remove friends or leave a guild without deleting your account, use the game's social screens. Questions: freddy.chia@trioncreation.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
