Bangkok needs its bloodlines

A rift tears open above the night market, and Shadow Yak giants pour out of the Himmapan forest into night markets, river piers and skytrain stations.

Ordinary young Bangkokians, a university student, a dancer, a Muay Thai fighter, a delivery rider, discover they carry the blood of Himmapan creatures. When their Bloodline awakens, they become giants.

Thai mythology as streetwear, not a museum. Every hero is a modern Bangkok character with a human form, a half-form and a giant Himmapan form.

Play a card. Hold the lane.

Short, readable battles with big mythic moments.

01

Three lanes, every card counts

Drag a hero card onto one of three lanes. Mana refills every few seconds, so send a tank to stop a brute, an archer to shoot down rift moths, or a healer to keep the push alive.

02

Go giant

Fill the Bloodline meter, tap a glowing hero and they become a giant Himmapan creature: Nam’s Naga Tide floods a lane, Sing’s Singha Roar stuns everything in front of him.

03

Duo Strikes

Put two partners in the same lane and unleash a Duo Strike, a combined finisher that can turn a losing push around.

04

30 heroes, 6 elements

Naga, Kinnaree, Singha, Krut, Hongsa, Makara, Kilen, Yak and more. Use the element wheel (Wind, Water, Fire, Earth, Light, Shadow) to hit weak spots, across five rarities.

05

Giant bosses

Every chapter ends with a three-phase giant boss with clear warnings: get out of the red, hit the glowing core and stop the charge before it reaches your gate.

06

Made for short sessions

Stages take 2 to 4 minutes. One-hand portrait controls, plays offline, and full Thai and English from day one.

Questions people ask

What is Himmapan Bloodlines?

Himmapan Bloodlines (Thai: สายเลือดหิมพานต์) is a card lane-defense game for iPhone, inspired by the Himmapan forest of Thai mythology. You drag hero cards into three lanes, hold back the Shadow Yak giants pouring out of rifts over Bangkok, and transform your heroes into giant Himmapan creatures such as the Naga, the Singha and the Krut.

Is Himmapan Bloodlines free?

Yes. The whole story, every stage, every event and the Daily Challenge are free to play. There are no ads and no energy timers. Optional purchases with fixed, listed contents save time or add style, and are never needed to win.

Is anything random for sale?

No. Nothing random is sold, for money or for gems. Every purchase shows exactly what you get. The only random reward is a free one earned by winning stages, and its odds are shown in the game. All 17 Normal and Rare heroes come free from the story, and every Legendary and Mythic hero can also be earned by playing.

When and where is it coming out?

Himmapan Bloodlines is coming in 2027 to the App Store, iPhone first. The App Store link will appear on this page when it goes live.

Which languages does it support?

Thai and English, in full, from day one. The Thai is written in casual spoken Thai for Thai players.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It is a single-player game that plays offline. Internet is only needed to buy, restore purchases and sync your save through your own iCloud.

Is it suitable for younger players?

The expected App Store rating is 9+ for mild cartoon fantasy violence with no blood. Players under 18 get a grown-up check before every purchase and monthly spending limits, and the game collects no personal data.

Who makes Himmapan Bloodlines?

Himmapan Bloodlines is designed, built and published by Trion Creation Sdn Bhd, an independent studio in Malaysia, as a love letter to Thai mythology and modern Bangkok.
