Questions people ask

Does missing a day reset my streak in Cadence?

No. Cadence counts a weekly goal instead of a daily chain, so missing one day still leaves the rest of the week to hit your goal. Your run continues instead of resetting to zero.

Is Cadence free?

Yes. The free tier keeps up to 10 habits with the full app working — mosaic, weekly goals, reminders and offline sync all included. Cadence Pro unlocks unlimited habits, every colour and theme, and a full year of mosaic history.

Does Cadence require a subscription?

No. Cadence Pro leads with a one-time lifetime purchase — pay once, own it forever. Annual and monthly options exist for people who'd rather spread the cost, but nobody has to subscribe to unlock Pro.

Does Cadence work offline?

Yes. The app is fully functional on your phone with no account. An account is optional and only exists to sync your habits between devices.

What platforms does Cadence support?

Cadence is available on Android now, on Google Play. An iOS version for the App Store is in progress.

Does Cadence have a home screen widget?

Yes. Android home screen and lock screen widgets let you check off a habit without opening the app.
