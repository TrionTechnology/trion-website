The streak is the problem

People don't quit habit apps because they stop caring. They quit because they miss one day, watch a number they were proud of drop to zero, and decide the whole thing was pointless.

Cadence sets a weekly goal. Four runs a week, not seven. Miss a Tuesday and you simply go on Wednesday — the goal is still met, the run continues, and nothing is taken away from you.

Built around one idea

An empty square is not a failure. It's just a day you didn't go.

The mosaic
