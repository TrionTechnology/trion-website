Privacy Policy

Himmapan Bloodlines / สายเลือดหิมพานต์
Publisher: Trion Creation Sdn Bhd ("we", "us"), Malaysia
Contact: freddy.chia@trioncreation.com
Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

You sign in with Google (and, on iPhone, with Apple once it is offered) to play, and your progress is saved on our server so it follows you between devices and so purchases and spending limits for players under 18 can be checked fairly. There are no ads, no tracking and no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data. We keep only what the game needs, on servers in Malaysia, for as long as your account exists. You can download your data (Settings → Download my data) or delete your account and everything with it (Settings → Delete account) at any time.

What we keep and why

Your account. A random player ID the server creates, your display name, the account's status and when it was created and last used.

Sign-in. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we store only the account identifier that Google or Apple gives the game for your account (a string of characters, not your email address), so we can find your save next time. We do not store your name, email address, photo or contacts. Google and Apple handle the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies.

Birth month and year. The game asks once, before play. We use it only to set the spending limits, the grown-up check and the other protections for players under 18, and to keep players under 13 off public leaderboards. It is never shown to other players.

Your save and progress. Your cloud save (stages, stars, heroes, deck, currencies, settings and the Glowfruit mode), the record of what you earned and spent in the game (the "ledger"), and your battle results. Battles are checked on the server against the game's rules; results that look impossible are recorded as anti-cheat notes.

Purchases. For every in-app purchase, the store's order or transaction ID, the product, the price in Thai baht, the time, and its status (delivered, restored or refunded). We verify purchases with Google Play or the App Store on our server so items are delivered once, refunds are honoured, and the monthly limits for players under 18 are counted correctly. We never see or store your payment card, wallet or billing details; Apple and Google process the payment.

Leaderboards. Your display name and score on the boards you take part in. Players under 13 never appear on public boards.

Security. Sign-in tokens, and server logs with your IP address and the request made, used to keep the service secure, limit abuse and fix problems. Tokens and sign-in secrets are removed from the logs.

On your device. The game also keeps a local copy of your save and your sign-in credentials in the phone's secure storage, so it can start quickly and play offline until it syncs.

Emails. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, attribution or advertising SDKs from third parties.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No chat and no user-generated content. Other players see only your display name and your scores.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data for marketing.

Unity engine. The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, ads, purchasing, cloud diagnostics, performance reporting and engine hardware statistics are all switched off, so the engine sends nothing to Unity Technologies from this game.

Who can see your data

  • Other players see your display name and leaderboard scores (never if you are under 13).
  • Our hosting provider, Alibaba Cloud, stores the data for us on servers in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. The game also downloads event and season settings from our content delivery network, which sees your IP address like any website. If you play from Thailand or another country, your data is transferred to and processed in Malaysia.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in and payments under their own privacy policies, and we send them purchase information only to verify and refund purchases.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, save, sign-in link, birth month and year, leaderboard entries and anti-cheat notes: while your account exists. When you delete your account, they are deleted straight away.
  • Battle results: 30 days. Anti-cheat notes: 180 days. The in-game earn-and-spend ledger: 90 days in the live database, then an archived copy in our Malaysian cloud storage for refund disputes and fraud checks.
  • Purchase records: after you delete your account they are unlinked from you (the player ID and store token are removed) and kept only as long as accounting, tax and refund rules require.
  • Server logs: as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then removed. Support emails: deleted within 12 months after the conversation ends, or sooner if you ask.

Deleting your account

  • In the game: Settings → Delete account (you confirm twice). This deletes your account and its data immediately. It cannot be undone, and deleting your account does not refund purchases; ask Apple or Google for refunds.
  • By email: write to freddy.chia@trioncreation.com from any address, with your display name and the store you used. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account. Details: Delete your account.

Children

Himmapan Bloodlines is rated 9+ and welcomes younger players, but the game needs a Google account to save progress. Players under 13 are asked to get a parent or guardian: a grown-up passes the grown-up check and signs the child in with the parent's or guardian's Google account. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13 on their own.

Players under 18 get a grown-up check before every purchase, monthly spending limits that are lower under 13, a limit on any single purchase, no offer countdowns, and (under 13) no place on public leaderboards. Parents can also use Google Play's purchase approval, Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

Parents: you can download or delete your child's data at any time with Settings → Download my data or Settings → Delete account, or by emailing freddy.chia@trioncreation.com.

Your rights

Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), Malaysia's PDPA 2010, the EU and UK GDPR and similar laws give you rights over your personal data:

  • Access and export: Settings → Download my data gives you a file with your account, sign-in link, save, purchases and recent ledger.
  • Correction: change your display name in Settings. Your birth month and year can be corrected after a 30-day lock, or by emailing us.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete account, or email us (30 days).
  • Objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent and complaints: email freddy.chia@trioncreation.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Thailand, the PDPC).

We answer within 30 days.

Security

All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in secrets live in your phone's secure storage, sign-in tokens expire, and purchases are verified with the stores on the server.

Changes

If we change what data the game handles, we will update this policy and the store privacy labels before that version is released, and change the date above.
