Privacy Policy

Himmapan Bloodlines / สายเลือดหิมพานต์
Publisher: Trion Creation Sdn Bhd, Malaysia
Contact: freddy.chia@trioncreation.com
Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

Himmapan Bloodlines is a single-player game that you can play offline. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your game data stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account, where we cannot see it.

What stays on your device (and in your iCloud)

The game saves your progress (stages, stars, heroes, deck, currencies, settings), a record of your purchases so items are delivered once, and, the first time you open the shop, your birth month and year. We ask for the birth month and year only to set spending limits and a grown-up check for players under 18.

This data is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the game, a copy is kept in your private iCloud storage so your progress and age setting follow you to your other devices. Apple stores it under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access your iCloud data, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game removes the copy on your device. You can delete the iCloud copy in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Purchases

The game is free. Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (or by Google through Google Play on Android). We never see or receive your name, email address, payment card, phone billing or wallet details, or your Apple ID. Apple tells the game only which item was bought (and when), so the game can deliver it, count it toward the monthly limit for players under 18, and restore it on your other devices. Apple's privacy policy applies to payments: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

What we do not do

  • No account or login, and no collection of names, emails, phone numbers or contacts.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or third-party data SDKs.
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts access.
  • No chat, user profiles or user-generated content.
  • No push notifications about offers.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete the conversation within 12 months after it is resolved, or sooner if you ask. We never add you to a mailing list.

Children and teenagers

The game is rated 9+. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (US COPPA) and minors under the laws of Thailand, Malaysia, the EU and the UK. For players under 18 the game adds a grown-up check before every purchase, monthly spending limits (lower for players under 13) and a cap on the size of any single purchase, and it never shows offer countdowns. Parents can also use Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time to approve or block purchases. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Laws such as Thailand's PDPA, Malaysia's PDPA 2010, the EU/UK GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights over your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing we hold to access, correct, export or delete, except emails you send us, which we delete on request.

Changes

If a future version changes what data the game handles, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released and change the effective date above.
