Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Himmapan Bloodlines / สายเลือดหิมพานต์ account and its data. The game is published by Trion Creation Sdn Bhd.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Himmapan Bloodlines and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

Your account and its data are deleted from our server immediately. The game signs you out and wipes the local save. Want a copy first? Tap Download my data before you delete.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the game)

  1. Email freddy.chia@trioncreation.com with the subject "Delete my Himmapan Bloodlines account".
  2. Include your in-game display name, the store you used (App Store or Google Play) and whether you signed in with Google or Apple. We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours; we never ask for your password.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • your player ID, display name and account status;
  • the Google or Apple sign-in link (the account identifier);
  • your birth month and year and the spending-limit settings;
  • your cloud save and all progress: stages, stars, heroes, deck, currencies, pass and event progress;
  • the in-game earn-and-spend ledger, your battle results and any anti-cheat notes;
  • your leaderboard entries;
  • your sign-in tokens, so no device can use the account again.

Deletion is permanent. It cannot be undone, and we cannot recover a deleted save.

What is kept

  • Purchase records (store order ID, product, amount, time): after deletion they are unlinked from you (your player ID and the store's purchase token are removed) and kept only as long as accounting, tax and refund rules require.
  • Server logs (IP address and request details) are kept for a short time for security, then removed. Archived ledger copies in our Malaysian cloud storage are kept for refund disputes and fraud checks.
  • If you email us, the conversation is deleted within 12 months after it ends.
  • Apple and Google keep their own record of your purchases under their policies.

Purchases and refunds

Deleting your account is not a refund. Unused Lantern Gems, an active Festival Pass or Lantern Card and any heroes or skins are lost with the account. Refund requests go to Apple (https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play, and must be made before you delete, because after deletion we can no longer match a refund to you. Questions: freddy.chia@trioncreation.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
