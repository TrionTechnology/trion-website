Delete your data

This page explains how to delete your Himmapan Bloodlines / สายเลือดหิมพานต์ data. The game is published by Trion Creation Sdn Bhd.

The short version

The game has no account, no sign-in and no server. Your progress, deck, settings, purchase record and the birth month and year from the age check are stored on your device and, if you turned on iCloud for the game, in your own private iCloud. We never receive them, so you delete them yourself, in a few taps, and nothing stays with us.

Option 1: on your device

  1. If the game offers Settings → Delete my data, use it: it erases the save on the device, including the birth month and year.
  2. Delete the app from your iPhone (press and hold the icon → Remove App → Delete App). This removes everything the game stored on the device.
  3. To remove the iCloud copy: open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, choose Himmapan Bloodlines and delete its data. (If iCloud was never turned on for the game, there is nothing to remove.)

After these steps your progress is gone for good. It cannot be recovered, by you or by us.

Option 2: by email

If you want us to check that we hold nothing about you, or to delete an email conversation you had with us, write to freddy.chia@trioncreation.com with the subject "Delete my Himmapan Bloodlines data". We reply and complete the request within 30 days. Emails you sent us are the only data we can hold; we delete them on request.

What is deleted

  • your stages, stars, heroes, deck, currencies and settings;
  • your in-game purchase record (the game's local list of what was delivered);
  • the birth month and year used for the under-18 spending limits;
  • the iCloud copy of all of the above, once you remove it in iCloud settings.

What is kept, and by whom

  • Apple keeps the record of your App Store purchases under its own policy. We cannot delete it, and you can still restore permanent items on a new install by signing in to the same Apple ID and tapping Restore purchases.
  • Deleting your data is not a refund. In-app purchases are handled by Apple; request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. Consumed Lantern Gems and finished pass seasons cannot be refunded through deletion.
  • We keep nothing else. Questions: freddy.chia@trioncreation.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
