Support

Contact: freddy.chia@trioncreation.com. Please include your device model, OS version, game version (Settings → About), your display name and what happened. We usually reply within 2 working days.

FAQ

Is the game free?
Yes. The whole story, every stage, every event and the Daily Challenge are free. There are no ads and no energy. Optional purchases (hero packs, the Festival Pass, the 30-Day Lantern Card, skins and Lantern Gems) save time or add style. They are never needed to win.

Is anything random for sale?
No. Every purchase shows exactly what you get. The only random reward is the free Glowfruit you earn by winning stages. Its odds are in Settings → Reward odds, and you can switch it to fixed rewards there.

Why do I have to sign in with Google?
Your progress, purchases and the spending limits for players under 18 live on our server, so they follow you to any device and cannot be lost with the phone. We store only Google's account identifier, never your email, name or photo. See the Privacy Policy.

I can't sign in / the game says the server is unreachable.
Check your connection and try again; a device that has signed in before keeps playing offline and syncs later. If it keeps failing, email us the time, your device and the message on screen.

I bought something but don't have it.
Tap Restore purchases in the Shop or Settings, signed in to the same store account you bought with. If that doesn't work, restart the game and try again, then email us the Order ID from https://reportaproblem.apple.com or your Google Play receipt. Never send your password.

How do I get a refund?
Apple and Google handle all payments. iOS: https://reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. A refunded purchase is removed from the account.

My child bought something.
Request a refund at the links above. Players under 18 always see a grown-up check before buying and have a monthly limit (lower under 13). To block purchases completely, turn on Google Play's purchase approval, or Apple's Ask to Buy / Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → In-app Purchases: Don't Allow.

My child is under 13. Can they play?
Yes, through a parent's or guardian's Google account: the game asks for a grown-up, who passes the grown-up check and signs in. Under-13 players don't appear on public leaderboards and have the strictest spending limits.

The game says "Monthly limit reached".
Players under 18 have a monthly spending limit. It resets on the 1st of each month. If the birth date was entered wrongly, it can be changed after 30 days.

Does the Festival Pass or the Lantern Card renew automatically?
No. Neither one ever renews or charges you again. The pass lasts for its 42-day season (plus 7 days to claim). The Lantern Card lasts 30 days, and missed days stay claimable for another 30 days.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Yes. Sign in with the same Google (or Apple) account on the new phone and your save is there.

How do I download or delete my data?
Settings → Download my data gives you a file with your account, save and purchases. Settings → Delete account deletes everything immediately and permanently; or see Delete your account.

Does it need internet?
To sign in, sync and buy, yes. Between syncs the game keeps playing offline and catches up when you're back online.

The screen flashes or shakes too much.
Settings → Reduce flashes and Reduce screen shake.

Can I make videos or stream the game?
Yes, including monetized videos. Tag #สายเลือดหิมพานต์ / #HimmapanBloodlines.

I found a cultural mistake or awkward Thai text.
Please tell us. We take Thai culture seriously and fix these quickly.
