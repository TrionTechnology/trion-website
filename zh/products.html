客户在项目启动前最常问的问题，我们直接给出答案。

Trion Creation 开发了哪些自有产品？

Trion Creation 目前发布的产品包括 Cadence、SoulSync AI。Cadence —— The habit tracker that forgives you. A habit tracker built on weekly goals instead of daily streaks. Missing a day doesn't reset your progress. Free tier keeps 10 habits with the full app working; Cadence Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase, no subscription required. Fully offline-capable. SoulSync AI —— Understand anyone you love. An AI relationship advisor that combines real Eastern metaphysics — Bazi (Four Pillars) and Zi Wei Dou Shu — with Western astrology, then uses AI coaching to explain the compatibility, dynamics and future potential of a relationship in plain language. Reads romantic partners, friendships, business partners and family.

Cadence 是什么？

Cadence 是由 Trion Creation Sdn Bhd 自主开发并发布的Health & Productivity类应用，支持 Android 和 iOS。A habit tracker built on weekly goals instead of daily streaks. Missing a day doesn't reset your progress. Free tier keeps 10 habits with the full app working; Cadence Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase, no subscription required. Fully offline-capable.

SoulSync AI 是什么？

SoulSync AI 是由 Trion Creation Sdn Bhd 自主开发并发布的Lifestyle & Relationships类应用，支持 Android 和 iOS。An AI relationship advisor that combines real Eastern metaphysics — Bazi (Four Pillars) and Zi Wei Dou Shu — with Western astrology, then uses AI coaching to explain the compatibility, dynamics and future potential of a relationship in plain language. Reads romantic partners, friendships, business partners and family.
