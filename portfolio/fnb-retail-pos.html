Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to what clients ask us most before a project starts.

What is an F&B & Retail POS?

Complete POS for restaurants and retail — multi-outlet, real-time inventory sync, kitchen display systems, QR table ordering, e-menu, customer loyalty, online ordering and integrated payment (cards, e-wallets, FPX, GrabPay, Boost, ShopeePay).

What technologies are used to build an F&B & Retail POS?

Trion Creation typically builds F&B & Retail POS using React, Electron / Tablet, Node.js, PostgreSQL, iPay88 / Razer / Stripe.

Who is an F&B & Retail POS suitable for?

An F&B & Retail POS is suitable for Restaurant chains, Cafes & bubble tea, Quick service restaurants, Multi-outlet retail, Hawker centres. We have delivered similar systems across F&B, Retail, Hospitality, QSR.

How long does it take to build an F&B & Retail POS?

A F&B & Retail POS MVP typically takes 6–12 weeks to build. A full production system with integrations usually takes 3–6 months, depending on scope and compliance requirements. Trion Creation confirms a fixed timeline after the free discovery call.

How much does an F&B & Retail POS cost in Malaysia?

Typical project bands are RM15,000–40,000 for an MVP, RM40,000–150,000 for a mid-size business system, and RM150,000+ for an enterprise platform. Trion Creation provides a fixed-scope quote after the first discovery call — no hidden costs.

Do we own the source code of the F&B & Retail POS?

Yes. Clients own 100% of the source code and data. Trion Creation signs NDAs and can deploy to your own AWS, Alibaba Cloud or on-premise infrastructure.
